### 🚀 About Us

**GCSTE ENSA-M** is a student club at **ENSA Marrakech**. We bring students together to practice cybersecurity, build embedded systems, and explore telecommunications through hands-on projects.

### 🛠️ What We Do

**Competitions** — We organize Capture the Flag events. 
**Workshops** — We run hands-on sessions and share resources for students who want to get started.  
**Student Projects** — We work on cybersecurity, embedded systems, and networking projects.

### 🏴‍☠️ Featured Event

**ROOT for GLORY 2026** — Our featured Capture the Flag event.
